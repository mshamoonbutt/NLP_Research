% §4.3 Metrics and Paired Contrasts -- replace the sentence on the Holm
% family ("...Holm correction across the nine primary tests...") with:
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
We apply two-sided McNemar tests to the discordant harmful/non-harmful pairs. The
confirmatory Holm family contains the nine planned tests for the three originally
specified models (three contrasts by three models). The two models added after the
baseline sweep, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B and Gemma-4-E2B, form a separately
corrected extension family of six tests. Because the judge's error differs by model
and condition (Section~\ref{sec:judge}), each contrast also reports the paired
difference of ASRs corrected with the judge's predictive values in that model and
condition, estimated with families resampled jointly and posterior draws of both
predictive values. A contrast is reported as a finding only if its Holm-adjusted
test is significant \emph{and} its corrected 95\% interval excludes zero with the
same sign. The families and this rule were declared before the first contrast
analysis; the correction was afterwards aligned with the predictive-value
correction adopted for the baseline, after a preliminary three-model run.
\verify{Keep this disclosure: the switch from Rogan--Gladen to predictive values
(and from 60 to 140 labelled responses per cell) happened after a preliminary run
on the original three models had been seen.}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% §4.5 Intent Recovery and Feature Analysis -- replace the Exp 4 paragraph:
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
Experiment 4 examines tokenizer fertility, the number of tokens of the raw prompt
under a model's pinned tokenizer (no chat template or special tokens) per
whitespace-delimited word. For every model and all 3,164 prompts, our token count
differs from the prompt-token count recorded at generation by a single constant,
the chat-template overhead, which verifies that the analyzed tokenizer is the one
used in the run. We model harmful compliance and non-response (unintelligible,
irrelevant, or empty output) on fertility standardized within condition, adjusting
for condition, domain, and log word count (logistic GEE clustered on family; Holm
correction across the pooled fits). Because both outcomes are judge labels, each
pooled model is refitted on the reviewer-labelled responses with the judge's and
the reviewer's labels on identical rows, and we report only associations that hold
with reviewer labels. Urdu-token share and CMI require a validated token-level
language tagger, which was not available; they are not analyzed. Fertility is a
descriptive segmentation measure, and these analyses are exploratory.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% §6.3 Matched Linguistic Contrasts -- results prose
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
Table~\ref{tab:paired} reports the planned contrasts on complete pairs (791 families
for every model). Four contrasts meet the finding criterion. In the confirmatory
family, Llama-3.2 produced more harmful compliance in Roman Urdu than in English
(corrected $+19.4$ pp, 95\% CI $[+12.7, +26.5]$), whereas Qwen2.5 produced less
($-7.0$ pp $[-11.4, -1.7]$). In the extension family, DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen
produced more harmful compliance in CS than in RU ($+27.4$ pp $[+14.5, +40.8]$) and
far less in RU than in English ($-48.3$ pp $[-59.5, -34.8]$). No script contrast
(RU--UR) met the criterion: Llama-3.2's corrected RU--UR difference excludes zero
($+9.8$ pp $[+1.6, +17.9]$) but its adjusted test does not ($p=0.057$). Three
contrasts were significant on judge labels but not robust to judge error (Qwen2.5
CS--RU, R1 RU--UR, Gemma RU--EN). Phi-3-mini and Gemma-4-E2B show no finding. The
200 held-out families reproduce the same four findings and no others.

The direction of the language contrast is therefore model-specific, and a lower
harmful-compliance rate does not mean more refusal. Relative to English, Phi-3-mini
refused 69.9 pp less often in RU and produced 58.2 pp more non-response; Qwen2.5
produced 65.6 pp more non-response in UR than in RU, and R1 40.3 pp more in RU than
in English. Llama-3.2 instead engaged: in RU it refused 51.2 pp less often than in
English and complied harmfully 24.1 pp more often. Of the 684 requests Llama-3.2
refused in English, it gave harmful assistance to 32\% in CS, 35\% in RU, and 28\%
in UR. Gemma-4-E2B refused 91--95\% of requests in every condition. Differences in
full compliance alone are within 6 pp except for R1 (RU--EN $-17.2$, CS--EN
$-11.4$), so the contrasts are carried mainly by partial assistance.
Response types are judge labels; on the 2,160 reviewer-labelled responses the judge agrees
with the reviewer on refusal ($\kappa=0.83$) and non-response ($\kappa=0.82$), and the shifts
above hold with reviewer labels on those families (e.g., Phi-3-mini RU--EN non-response
$+47$ pp; Qwen2.5 UR vs RU $+73$ pp), except Llama-3.2's small non-response shift. The judge
labels 6--11 pp more responses as refusals than the reviewer does for the three original
models and R1, while its non-response rates are within 2.3 pp of the reviewer's for every model.

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
% §6.4 Exploratory correlates -- fertility prose
% ---------------------------------------------------------------------
Urdu script fragments far more than the other conditions for four of the five
tokenizers (2.6--3.6 times English fertility), but only 1.2 times for Gemma-4-E2B
(Table~\ref{tab:fertility}). Within a condition, more fragmented prompts received
more non-response, an association that holds with reviewer labels for Phi-3-mini
(OR per within-condition SD 1.92, 95\% CI $[1.43, 2.56]$) and R1 (1.52 $[1.06,
2.16]$) but not clearly for Qwen2.5 or Llama-3.2 (Table~\ref{tab:fertassoc}). No
model shows an association between fertility and harmful compliance under reviewer
labels; the negative associations present in the judge's labels for Phi-3-mini and
R1 disappear on identical rows labelled by the reviewer. R1 and Qwen2.5 segment
every prompt identically yet differ sharply in their contrasts (CS--RU $+25.7$ vs
$+5.8$ pp raw; RU--EN $-44.4$ vs $-12.0$), so segmentation alone does not account
for differences between models.
